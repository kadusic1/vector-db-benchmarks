\section*{Sažetak}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Sažetak}

Rad predstavlja empirijsku evaluaciju četiri konfiguracije vektorskih
indeksa (Flat, IVF, IVF-PQ i HNSW) na skupu podataka MS~MARCO~v2.1,
korištenjem $500{.}000$ pasusa kodiranih u $768$-dimenzionalne vektore
modelom \textit{all-mpnet-base-v2}. Mjerene su latencija upita,
tačnost pretrage (\textit{Recall@10}) i memorijski otisak indeksa
uz $1{.}000$ upita po konfiguraciji. HNSW postiže najpovoljniji
kompromis između tačnosti ($0{,}9972$) i latencije (medijana
$4{,}451$\,ms), ali uz visoku varijabilnost u repovima distribucije
($P_{95} = 44{,}118$\,ms). IVF pruža konzistentno ponašanje
($8{,}576$\,ms, \textit{Recall@10}~$= 0{,}9564$), dok IVF-PQ
ostvaruje kompresiju od $40{,}88\times$ i najnižu latenciju
($1{,}117$\,ms) uz pad tačnosti od $33{,}21$ procentnih poena.
Hipoteza H1 je potvrđena, H2 djelimično, a H3 potvrđena.

% ---------- ENGLISH ----------
\section*{Abstract}

This paper presents an empirical evaluation of four vector index
configurations (Flat, IVF, IVF-PQ, and HNSW) on the MS~MARCO~v2.1
dataset, using $500{,}000$ passages encoded into $768$-dimensional
vectors with the \textit{all-mpnet-base-v2} model. Query latency,
search accuracy (\textit{Recall@10}), and memory footprint were
measured across $1{,}000$ queries per configuration. HNSW achieves
the best accuracy--latency trade-off ($0.9972$, median $4.451$\,ms),
but exhibits high tail latency ($P_{95} = 44.118$\,ms). IVF offers
consistent behaviour ($8.576$\,ms, \textit{Recall@10}~$= 0.9564$),
while IVF-PQ achieves $40.88\times$ compression and the lowest
latency ($1.117$\,ms) at a $33.21$ percentage point accuracy cost.
Hypothesis H1 is confirmed, H2 partially confirmed, and H3 confirmed.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:}
vector databases,
approximate nearest neighbour search,
HNSW, IVF, product quantization,
MS MARCO, embeddings,
information retrieval.
